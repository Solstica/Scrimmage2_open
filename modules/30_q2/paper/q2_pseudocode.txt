INPUT spectra at 10deg and 15deg
FOR each angle k
  convert reflectance percent to fraction
  keep 2500--3300 cm^-1
  define q_N = log10(N / 1 cm^-3)
  run 90 bounded stage-I least_squares fits over deterministic seeds
  sort stage-I solutions by RMSE
  deduplicate by key (round(d,3), round(q_N,2))
  retain the first eight distinct candidate solutions
  release seven Sellmeier parameters and refine the full 10-parameter model
  choose the candidate with minimum refined RMSE
END FOR
d_final <- (d_10deg + d_15deg) / 2
REPORT d_final, angle difference, half-range, RMSE, MAPE, R2, boundary hits
