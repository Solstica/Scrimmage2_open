INPUT spectra at 10deg and 15deg
FOR each angle k
  convert reflectance percent to fraction
  keep 2500--3300 cm^-1
  run bounded least_squares from deterministic coarse seeds
  retain eight distinct attraction basins
  refine each basin with Sellmeier perturbations released
  choose the feasible solution with minimum residual sum
END FOR
d_final <- (d_10deg + d_15deg) / 2
REPORT d_final, half_range, RMSE, R2, boundary_hits
